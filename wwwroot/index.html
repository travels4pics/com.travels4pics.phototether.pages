THE HOST APPWindows & macOS

The home base
for your photoshoot.

PhotoTether Host, named PhotoTether Lite in the app, receives camera uploads and brings your session together in one live gallery.

  • See the details. Zoom, pan, and inspect camera metadata alongside the image.
  • Find your frame. Filter by photographer, camera brand, model, or serial number.
  • Review at your pace. Lock an image while new uploads arrive, or keep the same zoom across frames.
  • Keep your originals. Files stay in your chosen work folder. Resume an existing photoshoot later.

THE COMPANION APPiPad & iPhone · iPadOS / iOS 16+

PhotoTether Mirror.
Take the gallery with you.

Give a client, collaborator, or yourself another view of the shoot. Mirror connects to a running PhotoTether host with a QR scan or the full viewer address.

  • Get a closer look. View full-resolution images with native pinch, pan, and double-tap zoom.
  • Make it your view. Use your own filters, lock a frame, and resize or hide the filmstrip.
  • Stay with the shoot. New photos arrive in the gallery, and the screen stays awake while the app is active.

Mirror is a viewer. It needs a running host on the same local network to receive photos.

BEFORE YOU CONNECT

Brand-independent.
Network-dependent.

PhotoTether uses FTP, so it isn’t tied to a camera brand. Compatibility comes down to what your camera can send over the network.

✓

A camera with FTP transfer

Your camera needs networking and the ability to upload photos using passive FTP (or supported FTPS). Wi-Fi alone doesn’t guarantee FTP support. Check your camera’s specifications.

✓

One reachable local network

The camera, host, and viewers must be able to reach each other. Guest-network isolation and firewalls can block connections. An internet connection isn’t required during a shoot.

✓

A computer with space for the shoot

Keep the host running and connected. You need at least 15 GB free to start; the host stops transfers if free space drops below 10 GB.

A few things to know.

This is a photo-transfer and viewing workflow. It doesn’t add networking to a camera, provide USB tethering, or remotely control the shutter.

JPEG is the simplest starting point. RAW previews depend on the available decoder and camera format; unsupported conversions preserve the original file. Transfer speed depends on your network and file size.

GOOD TO KNOW

A little more clarity.

Does PhotoTether need the internet?+

No internet connection is needed for a local photoshoot. The host, cameras, and viewers communicate over your local network. PhotoTether isn’t a cloud gallery or a remote internet-sharing service.

Can I use cameras from different brands?+

Yes, the host receives standard FTP uploads rather than relying on a brand-specific camera connection. Each camera must support the required network transfer settings. Filters can separate images using the camera and photographer metadata provided in the files.

Can Mirror work without the host?+

No. Mirror displays photos supplied by an active PhotoTether host on the same local network. If the host stops or restarts, reconnect Mirror using the current viewer address or QR code.

Can I protect a session?+

The host offers optional FTP credentials, FTPS for camera transfers, and HTTPS for viewing. Protected sessions use a viewer access key included in the QR code and address. FTPS and HTTPS require compatible clients and certificate setup; a password alone does not encrypt traffic.
