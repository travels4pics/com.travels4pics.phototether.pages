YOUR PHOTOS STAY YOURS

Privacy policy.

This policy applies to PhotoTether Host (PhotoTether Lite), its browser viewer, and PhotoTether Mirror, provided by Travels4Pics.

Effective October 30, 2026

Local storage. Local-network sharing. No analytics or tracking.

No data collection or analytics

We do not collect personal data or user photos through PhotoTether. The apps do not contain analytics or tracking, and do not send usage information, photos, or other user data to us or to remote servers.

Your data stays on your devices

PhotoTether processes photos and their metadata locally to provide its gallery and viewing features. Photos, session information, settings, and diagnostic logs are stored on the local device. We do not receive this information automatically.

The apps transmit data only within your local network: from cameras to the host through FTP or FTPS, and from the host to the browser viewer or PhotoTether Mirror. These transfers make your photos available to the devices you connect to your photoshoot; they do not upload them to a cloud service or remote server.

You control files saved in your selected photoshoot folder and can delete them using your device’s file tools. Deleting files on one device does not delete copies you have saved on other devices.

You retain your rights

We claim no ownership of your photos, other content, or intellectual property rights. Using PhotoTether does not transfer ownership of your content to us.

If you contact support

If you choose to email us, we receive the email address and information you include so we can respond to your request. This is separate from the apps’ local operation; PhotoTether does not automatically send support messages or attachments.

Privacy questions

Contact us at support.app.phototether@travels4pics.com with questions about this policy.
